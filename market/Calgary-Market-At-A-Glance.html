Key Takeaways

  • The city sits at 3.6 months of supply — squarely balanced, and slightly tighter than last week as 1,689 sales cleared against 6,142 active listings.
  • Detached and semi-detached are both at 3.0 months, the tightest categories in the city and right on the edge of seller's territory. Both moved 34% of their inventory in 30 days.
  • Condos remain the only buyer's market. At 5.9 months and a 17% sell-through, apartments are 31% of everything for sale but only 19% of what actually sold.
  • The $400K–$600K band carries the market: 496 of 1,689 city-wide sales — 29% — came from that price window across all four property types.
  • Sales pace has cooled three years running: July average daily sales went 77 (2024) → 68 (2025) → 61 (2026), down 21% over two years.
  • Luxury condos are frozen: 21 active listings over $1.5M produced a single sale — 21 months of supply. Condos at $600K–$700K aren't much better at 8.5 MOS, while detached over $1M is moving briskly at 2.7.
