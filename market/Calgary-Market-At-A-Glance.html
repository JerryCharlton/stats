Key Takeaways

  • Semi-detached is the segment to watch, with months of supply edging up from 3.0 to 3.1 — still Balanced but nudging toward the seller's line — while its $400K–$500K band leads the entire city at 47% sold.
  • Condos remain the city's only buyer's market at 5.0 months, but kept improving — supply eased from 5.1 as sales climbed to 352, led by the $200K–$300K tier (158 sales, 22% sold).
  • City-wide inventory grew this week, active listings up 27 to 6,037, while sales rose 10 to 1,695 — leaving overall supply unchanged at 3.6 months.
  • Sales velocity is still cooling year over year: August averaged 54 sales per day, down from 64 in August 2025 and 70 in August 2024 — a 23% decline over two years.
  • Luxury condos remain frozen for a second straight week — 22 active listings over $1.5M, zero sales — while $600K–$700K townhouses kept moving briskly at 38%.
