Key Takeaways

  • The city sits at 3.6 months of supply — balanced on paper, but the citywide average hides a wide split between houses and apartments.
  • Condos are the clear buyer's market: 5.6 months of supply and an 18% sell-through. Apartments make up 31% of all active listings but only 20% of sales.
  • Semi-detached is the tightest category at 2.8 MOS — the only type still leaning seller's, with 182 sales against 518 listings. The $400K–$500K tier sold 46% of its inventory in 30 days.
  • The $400K–$600K band does the heavy lifting: 510 of 1,727 city-wide sales (30%) came from that price window across all property types.
  • Sales pace has cooled three years running: July average daily sales went 77 (2024) → 68 (2025) → 61 (2026), a 21% drop over two years.
  • Luxury condos are frozen: 21 active listings over $1.5M produced a single sale in 30 days — 21 months of supply, the most stalled segment in the city. Detached over $900K is also slowing at 4.1 MOS.
