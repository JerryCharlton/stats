Key Takeaways

  • Calgary holds at 3.7 months of supply — squarely balanced. Inventory rose to 6,281 active listings while 1,699 homes sold over the last 30 days, a slight loosening from last week.
  • Detached and semi-detached are the tightest markets at 3.0 months. Both cleared 33% of their standing inventory in 30 days and sit right on the edge of seller's territory.
  • Condos crossed into 6.0 months of supply — the only buyer's market in the city and the only property type to soften this week. Apartments are 30% of everything listed but just 19% of what sold.
  • The $400K–$600K band still carries the market: 499 of 1,699 city-wide sales — 29% — came from that window across all four property types.
  • Sales pace has cooled three years running: July average daily sales went 77 (2024) → 68 (2025) → 61 (2026), down 21% in two years.
  • Luxury condos are fully stalled while luxury houses keep moving: 22 active condos over $1.5M produced zero sales in 30 days. Meanwhile detached homes from $1M–$1.5M sold 111 units at 2.8 months of supply — faster than the city average.
