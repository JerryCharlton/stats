Week over week, active listings rose 33 to 6,229 while sales rose faster — up 47 to 1,667 — nudging city-wide supply down from 3.8 to 3.7 months. Condos led the improvement, easing from 6.0 to 5.7 months of supply on 15 more sales. Detached, semi-detached and townhouse each ticked up modestly but held their market tags.

Key Takeaways

  • Supply tightened to 3.7 months, down from 3.8 last week — sales rose 47 to 1,667, outpacing a 33-listing rise in inventory.
  • Condos are still the city's only buyer's market at 5.7 months, but improving — MOS eased from 6.0 on 15 more sales week over week.
  • Semi-detached is the tightest segment at 3.1 months, and its $400K–$500K band is the fastest-moving price point in the city at 49% sold.
  • The $400K–$600K band keeps carrying the market: 485 of 1,667 city-wide sales — 29% — came from that window.
  • Luxury condos remain frozen — 23 active listings over $1.5M produced zero sales again — while detached $1M–$1.5M homes sold 116 units at 2.7 months of supply.
