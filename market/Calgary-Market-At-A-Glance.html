Key Takeaways

  • The city is at 3.7 months of supply — still balanced, but drifting up from 3.6 a week ago as sales ease faster than listings.
  • Every house category is now balanced; condos stand alone as the buyer's market. At 6.0 months and a 17% sell-through, apartments are 31% of active listings but only 20% of sales.
  • Semi-detached is still the tightest type at 3.0 MOS, with detached right behind at 3.1. The semi $400K–$500K tier moved 48% of its inventory in 30 days — the strongest sell-through in the city.
  • The $400K–$600K band carries the market: 479 of 1,641 city-wide sales (29%) came from that price window across all property types.
  • Sales pace has cooled three years running: July average daily sales went 77 (2024) → 68 (2025) → 61 (2026), a 21% drop over two years.
  • Luxury condos are frozen: 21 active listings over $1.5M produced one sale — 21 months of supply. Condos in the $600K–$700K tier aren't much better at 11.0 MOS, and detached $900K–$1M has softened to 4.3.
