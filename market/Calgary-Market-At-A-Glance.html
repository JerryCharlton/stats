Key Takeaways

  • Calgary slipped to 3.8 months of supply — still balanced, but loosening. Inventory fell to 6,196 active listings and sales fell faster, to 1,620 over the last 30 days. Fewer listings and fewer buyers at the same time.
  • Detached did all the softening this week. Sales dropped from 913 to 854 and supply moved 3.0 → 3.2 months. Semi-detached is now the tightest property type in the city at 3.1 months and a 32% sell-through.
  • Condos remain the only buyer's market at 6.0 months. Apartments are 30% of everything listed but only 19% of what sold — the same gap as last week, with no sign of closing.
  • The $400K–$600K band still carries the market: 473 of 1,620 city-wide sales — 29% — came from that window across all four property types.
  • Sales pace has cooled three years running: July average daily sales went 77 (2024) → 68 (2025) → 61 (2026), down 21% in two years.
  • Luxury condos are frozen while luxury houses keep moving: 23 active condos over $1.5M produced zero sales in 30 days. Detached homes from $1M–$1.5M sold 110 units at 2.8 months of supply — faster than the city average.
