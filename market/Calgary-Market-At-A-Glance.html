Key Takeaways

  • Semi-detached flipped to a seller's market this week, with months of supply dropping from 3.1 to 2.9 — now the tightest segment in the city, led by its $400K–$500K band selling 51% of active stock.
  • Condos are still the city's only buyer's market at 5.3 months, but improving — supply eased from 5.7 on more sales (334 vs 328 last week).
  • City-wide inventory contracted again, active listings down 293 to 5,936, while sales held nearly flat at 1,666 — tightening overall supply to 3.6 months.
  • Sales velocity is cooling seasonally: August averaged 54 sales per day, down from 64 in August 2025 and 70 in August 2024 — a 23% decline over two years.
  • Luxury condos remain frozen — 22 active listings over $1.5M produced zero sales again this month — while townhouse $600K–$700K sold at a brisk 39%.
